How it works

A microcontroller samples a sensor on a fixed interval, packages the reading, and publishes it to a cloud endpoint over Wi-Fi. A dashboard subscribes to the feed and renders it in near-real-time.

  • Wake on timer, take a reading, timestamp it.
  • Publish the payload to the platform over a secure connection.
  • Dashboard visualizes the stream and fires an alert past a threshold.
