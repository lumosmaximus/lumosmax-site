How I work

A few rules I keep coming back to

Bound the damage first.

Local-first logging, atomic state files, watchdogs and recovery paths before features. A gateway that loses its link should lose nothing, and tell you about it when it is back.

Measure before you guess.

Field logs, bench tests, traces and cycle counters. The slow or broken part is rarely where it feels like it is; the fleet's alerting and the watch's 315 to 107 cycles per pixel both came from looking at the numbers first.

Qualify, do not assume.

Replacement parts, vendor claims and AI-generated register maps all get checked against live reads and bench tests before anything goes to production.

Write it down, with the data.

Procedures, configuration standards and write-ups that keep the raw evidence next to the conclusion, so the next engineer, often me, can check the reasoning.
